Left open — your call

Slide 2 overflows by 21px at 1280×720

Six of those pixels sit past the section edge. It is the one slide you kept deliberately, so I did not touch it — but the overflow is a bug, not a design choice, and it predates this work.

Slide 4 is still a PNG

Against the no-raster rule you set at the start. You called it good, so it stayed; the rule and the slide still disagree.

Slide 7's narrow fallback does not stack

Its inline grid-column values are not overridden below 1000px, so three columns survive into a width that cannot hold them. Slide 27 was fixed; slide 7 was out of scope for the agent that found it.

The 1240px wrap cap against a vh-derived type scale

On a taller screen the type grows about 35% while the column does not, so prose-dense slides gain lines exactly where the stage has least slack. Deck-wide, and the reason two slides had to drop a heading size to fit.

An opener that names its own artifact

You rejected tasks.md on 13 and verification.md on 17. Slide 8 still ends in PLAN.md. Defensible — Plan's argument is that it produces a written artifact — but it is the one place the three openers disagree.

The full record of what was measured, what the plan got wrong, and the bugs found in the shipping deck is in presentation-v2/DESIGN.md.
