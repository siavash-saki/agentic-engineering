Show them the surface
they already work in.

The audience spends its day reading agent sessions. So the deck adopts that surface: a persistent session bar, content blocks typed by role with a coloured spine, paths and file names in monospace cyan, verdicts in green. The metaphor is not decoration — it maps one-to-one onto the content, because half the slides really are about transcripts, diffs and tool calls.

Best for
A dev-heavy room, a dim conference stage, or a recording. Instantly signals "this is for practitioners".
Costs you
Dark decks lose contrast in a bright meeting room, and photocopy badly. Needs a light print variant.
Risk taken
Four semantic accent colours rather than one — each carries meaning and must be used consistently.
