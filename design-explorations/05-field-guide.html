A handbook people
would actually keep.

The warmest of the five, and the closest to where the deck already stands — but disciplined where the current version is loose. Three fixed elevation levels, used to mean something: flat paper for ground, one card depth for peers, a deeper depth reserved for the answer in any comparison. Navigation is a plain eyebrow rail — chapter on the left, position on the right — so every slide is placeable without a heavy header. Monospace appears only where the content is literally code.

Best for
A mixed audience — engineers plus leads. Approachable, low-drama, easy to hand out as a PDF afterwards.
Costs you
The least distinctive. It reads as a very good deck rather than as a memorable one.
Risk taken
The smallest. Depth carries meaning instead of decoration — that is the whole discipline here.
