Plan before you build —
and let the slides prove it.

Every sheet is a technical drawing. Boxes are stroked outlines with corner ticks rather than filled cards, related parts are joined by dimension lines, notes hang off leader lines, and each slide closes with a real drawing title block: sheet number, chapter, revision. The deck's central claim — that a specification comes before a keystroke — is carried by the format itself, not just the words.

Best for
The spec-driven chapter especially. Makes process diagrams and workflows feel native rather than bolted on.
Costs you
The strongest metaphor of the five. If you don't commit to it on every slide, it reads as a gimmick.
Risk taken
Outlines everywhere, fills almost nowhere. Depth comes from line weight, not shadow.
